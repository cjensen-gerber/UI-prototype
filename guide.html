Getting orders unstuck with the Order Assistant

The Order Assistant finds out why an order is stuck between SAP and Deposco, and tells you exactly what to do next.

What the assistant is for

Use it whenever an order won't drop, allocate, pick, pack or ship, or when the two systems seem to disagree. It's good at problems like these:

What you see on the floorWhat it usually finds
The order isn't in DeposcoSAP tried to send it, but the send failed
The order won't allocateStock is on hold in Deposco but free in SAP
The pick quantity looks wrongA pack size (for example, 1 pack = 5 each) is missing in Deposco
The packing list doesn't match the orderThe order was changed in SAP after it was released to a wave
The shipping label won't printThe address was corrected in SAP but not in Deposco
What it never does: it looks things up in SAP and Deposco, but it doesn't change either system on its own. Any change is made by you or by the team that owns that system, and the assistant tells you who that is.

Examples in this guide use the prototype's made-up orders (SO-100412 and so on). Real orders will look the same.

How to start

Tap New issue, then give it the order number and what you see, in one line. The order number is the fastest route, because it's the first thing the assistant looks up.

Good startWhy it works
"SO-100437 won't allocate"Order number plus the symptom: it can start checking straight away
"Label won't print for SO-100479, UPS says address error"Includes the error message you saw
"Pick qty looks wrong on SO-100455, screen says 10 each"Says what the screen shows, so it can compare
Weaker startWhat happens
"Help" or "something's wrong"It asks which kind of problem you have, which adds a step
"The pink bodysuits are short"It needs the order number before it can look anything up

No order number to hand? Just describe the problem. It will ask for the number, and you can type it or scan it from the pick ticket.

Answering its questions

The assistant asks a few short questions while it checks the systems. Each answer changes what it looks at or how urgent it treats the order, so a quick, accurate answer gets you a better fix.

  • Tap a suggested answer to save typing, or type your own if none fits.
  • Say what you actually see. If you haven't checked the bin, tap "Haven't checked" rather than guessing.
  • Common questions:
    • Does it ship today? This sets the priority if it needs to escalate.
    • Which line is the problem?
    • What does the screen or error message say?
    • How far along is the order (picking, at pack, packed)?
  • Under What I checked, it lists each lookup as it goes, so you can see it's working.

Reading the diagnosis card

The diagnosis card shows SAP and Deposco side by side, so you can see exactly where they disagree. SAP is always on the left and Deposco on the right.

Part of the cardWhat it tells you
Title and severity tagThe problem in a few words, and how it affects the order, for example "Can't allocate" or "Blocked at label"
Field rowsThe same fact in each system. Rows marked ≠ (shaded red) are the mismatch; rows marked = agree.
Why it happenedA short plain-language explanation of the root cause
Who can fix itYou, a shift lead, or a system owner team such as Quality, SAP order support or Deposco admin

Example, for an order that won't allocate:

FieldSAPDeposco≠/=Match
Ordered30 EA30 EA · 12 allocated≠
On hand48 EA48 EA=
Available to allocate48 EA unrestricted12 EA≠
Hold statusNoneInspection hold since 28 Sep≠

Here the stock exists in both systems, but Deposco still has 36 units on an inspection hold, so it can only allocate 12.

Following the steps

After the card, the assistant gives you a numbered list of steps to fix the order. Do them in order, because later steps depend on earlier ones.

  • The label on each step says who does it. You means you, while Shift lead, Quality team or SAP order support means that person or team. Don't attempt steps that aren't yours; your login may not allow them, and that's on purpose.
  • The grey path shows where to go, for example Deposco › Waves › W-2287 › Re-allocate.
  • Tick each step as you finish it, so you (and the next shift) can see where you got to.
  • Tell it whether that fixed it. "Yes, it's fixed" closes the issue and adds it to the fix log, which helps the system owners stop it happening again. "No, escalate" opens a ticket for you.

If a step doesn't match what you see on screen, stop and tell the assistant what you see. Don't improvise around it.

Escalating

Escalate when a step needs another team, or when the steps didn't fix it. The assistant writes the ticket for you, so you don't have to retype anything.

  1. Tap Escalate at the top of the conversation, or answer "No, escalate".
  2. Check the draft. It already names the right team, the order, what the assistant found and what was tried.
  3. Add anything you saw that isn't in it, such as a carton count or a photo reference.
  4. Check the priority. It's set to High automatically when the order ships today.
  5. Tap Send escalation. The issue shows as Escalated in your list.

The owner team gets the ticket with the evidence attached. In the Teams version, you get a message in your chat when they pick it up and when they mark it fixed. Each owner team sets its own reply targets. The prototype shows about 30 minutes for high priority and 4 hours for normal, but treat those as placeholders until the teams confirm them.

Worked example: an order that won't allocate

This is the prototype's SO-100437, start to finish, in about two minutes.

  1. You: "SO-100437 won't allocate."
  2. Assistant: loads the order (3 lines, wave W-2287) and asks which line won't allocate. You tap "Line 10 · 2210-3M-PNK".
  3. Assistant: asks whether you've checked pick location A-14-03. You tap "Bin is empty".
  4. Assistant: compares SAP stock with Deposco, checks holds, and finds the receipt for PO 4500082231.
  5. Diagnosis card: "Stock is on an inspection hold in Deposco". SAP shows 48 units unrestricted, while Deposco has 36 on hold in QA-HOLD-01. Who can fix it: Quality team.
  6. Steps:
    1. You confirm 6 cartons in QA-HOLD-01 and check for green inspection tags.
    2. Quality team release the hold in Deposco.
    3. You re-allocate the order from the wave.
  7. You tap Escalate. The ticket goes to the Quality team marked High because the order ships today, and the issue shows as Escalated in your list.

Tips and quick reference

DoDon't
Start with the order number and the symptomStart with "help" and wait
Copy error messages word for wordParaphrase an error you half-remember
Answer "Haven't checked" when you haven'tGuess, which can send it down the wrong path
Stick to steps labelled "You"Try steps meant for another team
Tick steps off and confirm the fixLeave issues open after the order ships
Use order, delivery, SKU and bin numbersType customer names, addresses or phone numbers; the order number is enough

One order per issue. For a second order, tap New issue so each fix is logged separately.

Results reflect the latest data copy. The assistant reads copies of SAP and Deposco that refresh regularly. If something changed in the last few minutes, wait a moment and ask again.

Getting help: for problems with the assistant itself, contact your shift lead or the warehouse systems team. For anything urgent on a shipping order, escalate from the conversation so the right team sees it straight away.
