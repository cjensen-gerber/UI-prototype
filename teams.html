What's different in Teams

  1. Answers to the assistant's questions are suggested replies above the message box. They disappear once you answer, as in Teams.
  2. Escalations open a Teams dialog and post a card to the owner team's channel under Warehouse support.
  3. Open that channel to play the system owner. Select Assign to me, then Mark fixed. The update arrives in the warehouse user's chat, in Activity and as a notification.
  4. Make the window narrow (under 700px) to see the phone layout.
